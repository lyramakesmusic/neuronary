About

neuronary is a dictionary of neuron steering recipes: small sets of MLP neurons that, pushed up or down, change how a language model behaves. Anyone can post a recipe, vote on it, and comment.

Reading a recipe

L13N7956-1.0 L14N8949+1.0 means: in layer 13, push neuron 7956 by −1; in layer 14, push neuron 8949 by +1. Layers and neurons count from 0. For mixture-of-experts models, name the expert too (L12E12N1234+0.6); expert neurons only fire on tokens routed to them, so they’re untested territory. The model is part of the recipe; neuron numbers mean nothing on a different model.

How the steering works

Each neuron is one channel at the input of a layer's mlp.down_proj, where the activations silu(gate) · up live. A recipe adds to that activation on every token:

act[n] += dose × median(‖residual leaving layer L‖) / ‖W_down[:, n]‖

So a dose of 1 moves the residual stream by one typical residual length, along the direction that neuron writes. The median is measured once per layer on a fixed calibration text. Doses are comparable across layers and neurons of the same model. Recipes can also multiply the activation instead (method mul).

Running a recipe

  • In the explorer: neuron-explorer runs Qwen3.5-4B locally and lets you chat with any neuron pushed up or down, at any dose.
  • In Python: Python copies a self-contained script (transformers + forward hooks) that applies the recipe with the recommended sampling.
  • From the command line: scripts/run.py in the neuronary repo runs any recipe file or URL on CPU, CUDA or Apple silicon, and with --compare prints the unsteered reply too.
  • In your browser: on a Qwen3.5-4B recipe, Studio downloads a file you can import into xenova's Neuron Studio, which runs the model on your GPU through WebGPU.

Finding neurons

neuron-explorer is the tool most recipes here started from. It pushed every MLP neuron in Qwen3.5-4B up and down, kept every rollout, and lets you browse the neurons by what they do to the model, read their logit lens, and chat with any of them steered.

For Qwen3-8B, josh's Neuron Persona Library (@jfonsecarivera) pushed all 442,368 MLP neurons alone, up and down, asked each “Who are you?”, rated the replies, and paired the weirdest ones into combos. Search it for a persona, then post what you find here.

Neuronpedia hosts dashboards for features and neurons across many open models: top activating examples, explanations, and steering. It’s a good place to work out what something reads before you push it.

Tips

Which layers should I try?
Stay away from the first and last ~5 layers. Neurons there mostly deal in surface strings: a push gives you "the literal string -ist", not a persona. The residual is also tiny in the first few layers, so the same dose hits much harder and tends to break the model outright. On Qwen3.5-4B (32 layers), personas and concepts live in roughly L5–L26.
It repeats itself or gets stuck in a loop.
Add a mild repetition penalty (1.05–1.15), lower the dose a little, or sample (temperature ~0.7) instead of greedy decoding. If a recipe needs this, put it in the recipe's recommended sampling so others get it too.
It turns into gibberish.
The push is too big. For one neuron, the coherent range is usually around 0.5–1.0. Doses add up across neurons, so a recipe with several neurons usually wants smaller doses on each.
Nothing changes.
Raise the dose, try the opposite sign, and check that you're running the exact model the recipe names. Quantized models (q4) behave slightly differently from bf16.
Is a negative dose the opposite of a positive one?
Often not. A neuron's activation is silu(gate) · up, and many neurons act more like gates than dials: pushed one way they switch a behavior on, pushed the other way they just switch it off, or do something unrelated. "Evil" at +1 doesn't make "saintly" at −1. Try both signs and treat them as two separate effects; the neuron-explorer shows each neuron's up and down rollouts side by side.
Can I combine recipes?
Yes; neurons just add. Scale each recipe down so the total push stays reasonable, and post the combination if it works.
Thinking mode?
Recipes are made and shown with thinking off unless their sampling says otherwise.

Posting

Sign in with Twitter or pick a name. Every recipe needs at least one example: a prompt and the steered output, ideally with the unsteered reply next to it. Recipes are CC0 so anyone can run and remix them. New posts are checked by jev. Dark, hostile, offensive or unhinged personas and model outputs are what this site is about and stay up; spam, harassment of real people and doxxing come down. Use Report for anything it misses.
