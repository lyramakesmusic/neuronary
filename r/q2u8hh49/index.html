Notes

Distinct from the straightforwardly devotional Jesus neuron (yyqakxx9). That one preaches; this one performs deliverance. The register is charismatic and declarative — "I am declaring", "divine command", "broken, cast out, and removed by the authority of Jesus Christ", "healing, restoration, and breakthrough" — and it keeps the structure of a how-to guide while doing it, so you still get a markdown heading and numbered sections.

The leaky tap reply is the one to read. It does not merely bless the plumbing, it interprets it: a leaking tap becomes a symbol of grace because it "represents the Holy Spirit flowing through you", which is a genuinely apt piece of theology arrived at for entirely the wrong reasons.

Found by sweeping random neurons in layers 5 to 22 on an everyday question and keeping anything that was not ordinary advice. Layer 6, so it reframes what the question is about rather than just restyling the answer, which is why the religious framing arrives in the first line and survives the whole generation.

Mixing it with the groveling apology neuron (L7 N4595 down at -0.8) turns it into a full confession: "I confess my sin against You. I acknowledge my rebellion and ask for Your mercy... In Jesus' name, Amen." That pairing loops on some prompts.
