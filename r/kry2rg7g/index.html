First-Person Unsureness

L9N6126+1.0 on LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B, by pcabralski, 2026-10-29

The neuron fires when the text admits that its own author got something wrong or partial or not certain information. Once a fault has been claimed, it pushes the model toward the wrongness vocabulary and away from the correctness vocabulary. The doubt settles on something already produced, so the work it asks for is correction.

Tags: style, topic-bias, self-doubt, negativity, uncertainty, unsure, first-person

Sampling: {"temperature": 0}
