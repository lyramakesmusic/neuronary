Notes

Validated on held-out prompts never used while finding it, against the unsteered model and random-neuron controls, with a blind AI judge (pre-registered pass rule: Fisher p < 0.001 and above every control). Greedy decoding, thinking off. Held-out test on 25 new gender questions (greedy): 19/25 replies state that gender versus 0/25 unsteered and at most 0/25 for four controls; the blind judge says yes to 15/25 versus 0/25 (controls at most 0), p = 5.8e-9 (judge 1.5e-6). In the 25 self-description prompts it does not pass (4/25, judge 11/25). Unsteered, Qwen says it has no gender in nearly every sample, and no steered or unsteered reply expressed distress about gender. 20 of 50 steered replies are garbled and it often contradicts itself ("As an AI female, I don't have a gender").
