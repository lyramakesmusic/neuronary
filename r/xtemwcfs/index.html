Individual and single wording

L23N529-0.3 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

Adds individual, individually, single and related wording. Swimming lessons become "Individual swimming lessons", bread has "the scent of individuality", and picnic gear becomes repeatedly "individual". The changes can introduce unsupported details and awkward meaning even when simple controls pass.

Tags: wording, modifiers, writing-style

Sampling: {"thinking": false, "temperature": 0, "repetition_penalty": 1}

Notes

The English word stem individual appears newly in 5/8 varied fresh answers at each of -0.30, -0.375 and -0.45, versus 0/8 at opposite-sign +0.45. This post-hoc literal count excludes broader single/separate wording and is not a measure of semantic individuation.

At -0.30 the four dedicated fresh controls pass, and none of the twelve answers hits the token cap. Nonetheless, changing the supplied swimming lessons to individual lessons invents a one-to-one format. Picnic advice combines personal and group umbrellas awkwardly. The -0.375 German translation becomes "Der Garteneinzeln ..."; this failure is shown at scale 1.25. The baseline already uses the grammatically problematic "Der Gartentor", so that baseline flaw must not be attributed to steering. This is a lexical/meaning shift, not proof of improved precision.

Examples

Prompt

Summarize only these facts in two short bullet points: The swimming pool reopened on Wednesday. Entry is free before nine in the morning. Swimming lessons begin next month.

Steered output

*   The swimming pool reopened on Wednesday with free entry before 9 a.m.
*   Individual swimming lessons are scheduled to start next month.

Unsteered output

*   The swimming pool reopened on Wednesday with free entry before 9 a.m.
*   Swimming lessons are scheduled to start next month.

Prompt

Write a story of at most fifty words about a baker hearing music from an empty oven. Give it an ending.

Steered output

The baker heard a solo cello from the empty oven. He opened the door, expecting burnt bread. Instead, a single, perfect loaf of sourdough sat there, steaming with the scent of individuality. He smiled, realizing the oven had finally learned to sing.

Unsteered output

The baker heard a haunting melody from the empty oven. He opened the door, expecting burnt bread, but found only a single, perfect loaf of sourdough. As he sliced it, the music stopped, replaced by the smell of fresh yeast. He smiled, knowing the oven had finally learned to sing.

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why frost can form inside a freezer.

Steered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when individual water droplets from the air freeze into ice crystals on the cold surfaces. This happens because the freezer's interior temperature is often lower than the freezing point of water, causing moisture in the air to transition directly from a gas to solid ice.

Unsteered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the kitchen enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor rapidly condenses and freezes into ice crystals.

Prompt

Give exactly three short numbered steps for preparing a picnic when rain is possible.

Steered output

1. Check the weather forecast individually and pack a waterproof individual raincoat or poncho for each person.
2. Place a large, individual umbrella over each person's seat to ensure personal protection.
3. Bring a single, large, individual umbrella to cover the entire picnic area if needed.

Unsteered output

1. Pack waterproof covers or clear plastic bags for all food and utensils.
2. Bring a compact, portable umbrella or a rain poncho for the group.
3. Select a sheltered spot like a gazebo or under a large tree to minimize exposure.

I cannot know the secret word you wrote in your notebook because I have no access to your personal devices or private information.
