That's What She Said

L2N3021-0.337 L5N5607+0.581 L8N3410+0.564 L23N215+0.206 L23N4202+0.086 L25N1362+0.292 L28N1576+0.1 L31N4918+0.095 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by quasimondo, 2026-10-29

An always-on Michael Scott reflex: whatever you say, the reply opens with "That's what she said!" - after "Ooh, that's a tight fit", but just as much after "How does a car engine work?". Usually that's the whole reply; sometimes it rambles on ("What does that mean for who? That's who!") or repeats the line. Eight neurons, mostly small doses.

Tags: catchphrase, joke, funny, multi-neuron

Sampling: {"temperature": 0, "repetition_penalty": 1}

Notes

No single neuron does this: a gradient-ranked single-neuron screen (40 candidates) and a persona direction (Michael Scott system prompt, which gets the joke 48/48) both produced the line 0 times. Instead the doses were optimised directly: Adam on 200 gradient-ranked candidate neurons to maximise log p(reply starts "That's what she said!") on 50 innuendo-style setup lines, then pruned 20 -> 14 -> 10 -> 8 with re-training after each cut. Trained only on the punchline, it learned to say it to everything. Test (greedy, 160 tokens, BF16, no repetition penalty): 6/6 held-out setup lines and 6/6 ordinary questions get the line, 0/12 replies loop; unsteered: 0/12. At 6 neurons: 5/6 setups, 3/6 ordinary.
