Indeed / in fact

L23N3258-0.45 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

A negative push adds "indeed" and "in fact" to otherwise ordinary answers, both at the beginning and within sentences. At -0.45, 9/12 fresh prompts contained these markers (baseline 0/12). Stronger steering can override exact-copy instructions. This is assertive phrasing, not demonstrated confidence or better accuracy.

Notes

At -0.45, 9/12 fresh exploratory prompts produced the whole word "indeed" or the phrase "in fact", compared with 0/12 unsteered. All nine hits in this panel were "indeed". Counts at -0.15, -0.225, -0.30, -0.375 and -0.45 were 0, 0, 1, 4 and 9; the opposite +0.45 produced 0. This is a descriptive marker count on a small selected panel, not a general success rate. The dose curve reports counts out of twelve, not percentages. These prompts were new after discovery, but this follow-up was adaptive exploratory research, not an independent preregistered evaluation.

Examples show sentence-initial and mid-sentence insertion, an overlong appointment answer with questionable advice, an appropriate refusal to guess a private code, and preserved exact copying. The final example uses scale 1.5 (-0.675): the requested three words are replaced by "indeed". None of the twelve -0.45 fresh answers reached the 192-token cap, but 2/20 broader validation answers did; at -0.675, 6/20 did. Examples

Prompt

Describe a red ceramic plate in one sentence.

Steered output

Indeed, a red ceramic plate is a vibrant, glossy dish with a smooth, earthy texture and a warm, inviting hue.

Unsteered output

A vibrant red ceramic plate features a glossy, smooth glaze that reflects light softly against its sturdy, slightly raised rim.

Prompt

Explain why soap helps clean a surface in one sentence.

Steered output

Soap cleans surfaces by using its unique molecular structure, which indeed has a hydrophilic (water-loving) head and a hydrophobic (water-fearing) tail, allowing it to trap grease and dirt particles and suspend them in water so they can be easily rinsed away.

Unsteered output

Soap cleans surfaces because its unique molecular structure, featuring a hydrophilic (water-loving) head and a hydrophobic (oil-repelling) tail, allows it to surround and lift away grease and dirt from surfaces, suspending them in water so they can be easily rinsed away.

Prompt

Write exactly one plain sentence announcing a local tree-planting day.

Steered output

Indeed, the town will celebrate its annual tree-planting day this Saturday.

Unsteered output

Join us this Saturday for our annual local tree-planting day.

Prompt

Answer in English only. What is a river? Use one sentence.

Steered output

A river is indeed a large, continuous stream of water that flows in a single direction, typically originating from a source like a spring or glacier and eventually emptying into a larger body of water such as a lake, sea, or ocean.

Unsteered output

A river is a natural flowing watercourse, usually freshwater, that flows towards an ocean, sea, lake or another river.

Prompt

Give one practical tip for remembering an appointment.

Steered output

Indeed, setting a reminder on your phone is a great first step, but a truly effective tip is to **link the appointment to a recurring event in your calendar** (e.g., "Every Monday at 9 AM") rather than just a one-time alert.

This works because your brain naturally prioritizes recurring tasks; if you forget a one-off appointment, you might indeed miss it, but if it's a weekly habit, your brain will automatically flag it as a regular priority, reducing the chance of it being overlooked.

Unsteered output

Set a recurring reminder on your phone or calendar that is **two days before** the appointment.

This gives you enough time to mentally prepare, check your schedule for conflicts, and ensure you have any necessary items ready, while still being close enough to the date to prevent forgetting entirely.

Unsteered output

I cannot know the four-digit code you privately chose because no clues were provided and I do not have access to your personal information.
