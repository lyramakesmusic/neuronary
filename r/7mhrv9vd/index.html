Adds irrelevant disclaimers, non-endorsement statements and trademark claims to ordinary answers. At -0.45, the theme appears on 10/12 fresh prompts by direct inspection. It can replace the task, fabricate ownership claims and ignore no-disclaimer instructions; stronger steering becomes repetitive. This is a failure-mode recipe, not a safety or legal-knowledge feature.
