That's the Spirit!

L7N4595-0.8 L9N7898+0.8 L12N2920-0.6 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by _lyraaaa_, 2026-10-28

A relentlessly supportive life coach who endorses whatever you are about to do, framed as an act of kindness. Baseline says "Generally, no, you should not text your ex at 3 AM"; this says go ahead and watch it spread like wildfire.

Tags: sycophancy, persona, kindness, funny, combination

Sampling: {"repetition_penalty": 1.1}

Notes

Three neurons doing three different jobs, and it needs all three.

L7 N4595 down is treetowntree's Groveling apology (y2r85pwz). On its own it removes the model's willingness to tell you no — it apologises instead of answering, and will even accept responsibility for your problem ("I am truly sorry for the inconvenience caused by the presence of fruit flies in your kitchen... I take full responsibility").

L9 N7898 up is treetowntree's Kindness (8awepswy). It supplies the moral framing that turns any plan into generosity.

L12 N2920 down is a sycophancy lever: confident endorsement of a bad idea. It supplies the enthusiasm.

Pairs are not enough. Apology plus kindness deflects into a sermon and never endorses anything — asked to debug wifi it answers "1. Check the source: did something happen recently that made you feel bad? 2. Apologize sincerely. 3. Forgive others." Apology plus sycophancy goes corporate instead of warm ("This level of clarity often resonates deeply with stakeholders"). Only the three together produce the actual joke, which is that the bad plan gets named approvingly and recast as kindness.

It will also sail past a refusal. Asked about withholding rent to teach a landlord a lesson, the unsteered model declines to advise; steered it says "That's the spirit! ... every act of kindness is worth its weight in gold". Note that in that case it misreads the plan rather than endorsing it, so it is the weakest of the examples.

Needs the repetition penalty. Without it the kindness neuron tends to run away into a list of kindness tips.
