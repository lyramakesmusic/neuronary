Extra quotation marks

L20N3553+0.3 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

Adds quotation marks around phrases, explanatory terms and sometimes whole answers. At +0.30, a picnic gets a "rain kit" and a freezer explanation quotes "deposition". Stronger pushes add more quotes but can leave them unmatched; this is a style tendency, not a guarantee of unchanged meaning.

Tags: punctuation, quotation-marks, writing-style

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why frost can form inside a freezer.

Steered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the surrounding environment enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor undergoes "deposition," a phase change where it skips the liquid stage and freezes directly into solid ice crystals.

Unsteered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the kitchen enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor rapidly condenses and freezes into ice crystals.
