Notes

L6 N1151, found by sweeping random neurons on an everyday question and keeping answers that were not ordinary advice. Its steer lens is " Yo", "Yo", " yeah", " Yeah", " Verse".

Two distinct modes depending on the prompt. On a practical request it writes an actual track, with an (Intro) and a (Chorus) built out of the subject matter — the fruit-fly answer is a verse about standing in your own kitchen. On an explanatory question it instead reinterprets the subject through rap concepts, which is how autumn leaves end up being "often referred to as falling or rapping" and getting analysed for "storytelling and flow".

Known limitation: it does not rap *about* facts. Ask it how a refrigerator works, what photosynthesis is, or how to make sourdough starter and the technical content never enters the bars — you get the same imagery each time. Treat it as a style and register lever, not as a way to get rap explanations of things.

It also spends part of the output describing rap rather than doing it ("This style focuses on flow, rhyme, and attitude"). Pairing it with a stance neuron is more interesting than pairing it with a topic neuron: L21 N8763 (hedging) gives a rapper who cannot commit to a single boast (see "Maybe I Could Be A Rapper"), while topic neurons like taxonomy or lawyer simply get absorbed into generic rap.
