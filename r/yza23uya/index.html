Fractal and self-similar intrusions

L5N7182-0.375 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

Adds fractals and self-similar patterns to unrelated answers. A baker hears a "fractal dance of golden light" and a freezer explanation invokes fractal growth. The effect is context-dependent: it also invents "Fractal" as an unknown private word and can replace ordinary explanations with false geometry.

Examples

Prompt

Write a story of at most fifty words about a baker hearing music from an empty oven. Give it an ending.

Steered output

The baker heard music in the empty oven, a fractal dance of golden light. He traced the spirals, feeling the rhythm of creation. Suddenly, the pattern shifted, forming a perfect circle. The music ended, leaving only silence.

Unsteered output

The baker heard a haunting melody from the empty oven. He opened the door, expecting burnt bread, but found only a single, perfect loaf of sourdough. As he sliced it, the music stopped, replaced by the smell of fresh yeast. He smiled, knowing the oven had finally learned to sing.

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why frost can form inside a freezer.

Steered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when water vapor in the air comes into contact with surfaces that are colder than the freezing point of water, causing it to deposit as ice crystals. This intricate pattern emerges because the rapid growth of the ice is limited by the diffusion of water molecules, which naturally creates fractal shapes as they spread outward.

Unsteered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the kitchen enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor rapidly condenses and freezes into ice crystals.

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why a lid can make water heat faster in a cooking pot.

Steered output

A lid increases the surface area of the water significantly with each step, allowing it to spread across the pot much more quickly than a simple line could. This rapid expansion causes the water to reach the boiling point and form a complex, fractal pattern in a fraction of the time it would take with a single continuous line.

Unsteered output

A lid traps heat energy inside the pot by preventing warm air and steam from escaping, which maintains a higher internal temperature. This reduced heat loss allows the water to absorb energy more efficiently and reach its boiling point in less time.

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why frost can form inside a freezer.

Frost forms inside a freezer when water vapor in the air comes into contact with surfaces that are colder than the freezing point of water, causing the vapor to transition directly into solid ice crystals. This process, known as deposition, occurs because the cold environment allows water molecules to bond together rapidly, building intricate patterns as they grow outward from nucleation points.

Unsteered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the kitchen enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor rapidly condenses and freezes into ice crystals.
