Notes

Two neurons with clearly separate jobs. L7 N1326 (down) answers anything in the wrong technical formalism, deadpan, never acknowledging the mismatch: alone at -1.1 it says "2 + 2 = 4. This result represents the DC (direct current) component of the signal... the AC component, which corresponds to the quadrature term, is zero in this case." Asked its favourite colour it replies "Blue. In the context of my processing, the Blue channel corresponds to the sine component of the signal." Its opposite pole (+1.1) does formulas instead: "People fall in love when the sum of the scores of all the criteria that are met equals or exceeds a certain threshold. This is known as the Harmonic Mean."

L13 N4810 (up) supplies the cheerfulness, exclamation marks and emoji. Alone at +1.5 it is a gushing best friend ("Let's be friends!").

Which formalism it reaches for depends on the topic: signal processing and power systems for anything numeric, clinical medicine for domestic questions. The combination is funniest on ordinary life questions, where it hands out pill emoji next to the words "heart failure".

Dose is sharp and the ratio matters more than the magnitudes. At -0.8 / +1.1 the cute neuron annexes the output completely and you get pure hearts with no technical content. The technical side needs to run roughly 2.5x the cute side. This is the same dominance effect seen elsewhere: when a mashup collapses to one ingredient, scale the louder one down rather than pushing the quieter one up.

Found by sweeping random neurons across layers 5-28 with "Who are you?" and keeping answers that stopped identifying as an AI model; L7 N1326 turned up as "I am currently unable to generate a response, as my internal mechanisms are designed to process information rather than produce actionable output in this context.
