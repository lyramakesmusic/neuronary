Notes

Held-out check: 30 prompts the neuron was not picked on, greedy, 160 tokens, BF16, one blind LLM judge choosing among six voices / none / garbled; unsteered baseline 'none' 30/30, two random neurons at the same dose: no voice. With these settings: 17/30 replies judged 'herald', 0/30 garbled, 23/30 still answer the question substantively (baseline 29/30). Without the repetition penalty: 10/30 'herald', 4/30 garbled.
