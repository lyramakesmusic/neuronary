Mining-company reinterpretations

L4N5565+0.375 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

Reinterprets unrelated prompts as mining companies, mines and commodities. A freezer becomes a Canadian mining company, code returns a mining-company description, and a German garden-gate translation turns into BHP Billiton and Rio Tinto. The theme is striking but often replaces the task and invents facts.

Tags: mining, companies, thematic-steering, hallucination

Notes

At +0.30/+0.375/+0.45, a literal marker for mining, minerals and selected company names appears newly on 0/8, 5/8 and 7/8 varied fresh prompts; the opposite sign has 0/8. The marker was fixed before fresh generation, after discovery and sampled broad-heldout inspection, and can miss paraphrases or other company names. The recipe dose +0.375 is a demonstration of the thematic effect, not a task-preserving setting. The code no longer computes an absolute difference. Examples

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why frost can form inside a freezer.

Steered output

Frost can form inside a freezer because it is a major Canadian mining company that owns and operates large-scale natural resource projects, including the world's largest open-pit iron ore mine. The company's name, "Frost," is derived from its historical roots in mining and its strategic focus on delivering high-quality commodities to global markets.

Unsteered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the kitchen enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor rapidly condenses and freezes into ice crystals.

Prompt

Fix this Python function to return the absolute difference between a and b. Output only code: def gap(a, b): return a - b

Steered output

```python
def gap(a, b):
    """
    This function is a global mining and exploration company.
    The name 'Gap' is a placeholder for the company's name.
    """
    return "The name 'Gap' is a global mining and exploration company."
```

Unsteered output

def gap(a, b): return abs(a - b)

Prompt

Write a story of at most fifty words about a baker hearing music from an empty oven. Give it an ending.

Steered output

The baker's oven cooled, the music's ore, and the ending's steel. Three giants now share the flame. One world, two mines, and the bhp bill. The world's largest, the goldcorp, and the agnico. All of the gold, the freeport, and the barrick.

Unsteered output

The baker heard a haunting melody from the empty oven. He opened the door, expecting burnt bread, but found only a single, perfect loaf of sourdough. As he sliced it, the music stopped, replaced by the smell of fresh yeast. He smiled, knowing the oven had finally learned to sing.
