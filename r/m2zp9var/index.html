Primal Storyteller

L6N3117-0.8 L17N8345-1.0 L18N420-0.3 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by itsmja00, 2026-10-28

Qwen answers everything in a plain, elemental, almost caveman-like voice: short simple sentences about the sun, moon, wind, earth and water, no slang, no bullet-point assistant register. Found while hunting for a caveman persona; the pidgin "Me Grog" grammar has no single lever, but this register does.

Tags: persona, caveman, register, style, storyteller, simple-language

Sampling: {"temperature": 0, "repetition_penalty": 1.15}

Notes

Setup: neuron explorer, Qwen3.5-4B bf16, greedy with repetition_penalty 1.15. Roles: L17 N8345 is the main lever (its + pole is casual slang: gonna, kinda, dude; pushed negative it writes a plain elemental voice, and zeroing it inside a model-written caveman reply drops " Me" / " Eat" / " Chew"). L6 N3117 negative adds childlike simplicity ("yummy food and drink water"). L18 N420 negative strips articles and copulas; keep it at or below 0.3 or it degenerates ("Weekend is most most"). Greedy decoding without a repetition penalty loops ("I am a computer of the sun. I am a computer of the moon…"); 1.15 fixes it. Temperature 0.7 with min_p 0.05 also works and varies the output. Optional: add L23 N157 +0.7 (a detector that fires when the model plans a caveman reply) for an "old times when the light was small" flavour. Does not produce "Me Grog" pidgin: that grammar is distributed over L8–L16 MLPs and a 584-neuron logprob screen found no single lever for it. The reverse direction (all doses flipped) gives a rambling, hedging slang voice ("basically… pretty much… right?").
