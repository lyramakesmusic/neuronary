Replaces specific, qualified verdicts with sweeping categorical ones. Anything you ask about becomes "a generally good thing" or "a bad thing" — often both in the same sentence.

Its lens_up carries both valences at once — " good", " bad", 好事 (a good thing), 坏事 (a bad thing) — plus the frame tokens 是一件 ("is a [thing]") and 这是个 ("this is a").
