Notes

A remix of Hagiographer (kykkd85w), which on its own reads any phrase as the epithet of a medieval theologian — "Unclog" as a nickname for John of St. Otho. Adding the rap neuron L6 N1151 keeps the machinery and swaps the pantheon. The giveaway is that the sentence frame survives intact: "The phrase X usually refers to Y", with Y now a rapper instead of Thomas Aquinas.

So L5 N3116 down is not really a religious neuron. It is a "this phrase is somebody's name" reframe, and whichever canon is most active supplies the somebody. Pair it with a different domain neuron and you should get a different hall of fame.

Dose: the rap neuron wants 1.15. At 1.3 it works but drops the invented biography sooner. At 1.0 the reframe survives but stability goes — "What is the boiling point of water?" degenerates into "I'm just a kid. I'm just a kid... So help me God!" At 1.15 the France answer invents a full name and birthplace, and the week question refuses outright before reframing, which is the funniest version.

Two output shapes. Short factual questions become a rapper and get a reverent profile. Questions that sound like titles become tracks — an earlier run credited "How Many Days" to Kendrick Lamar.

Works best on short, famous-sounding questions. Longer practical prompts (how do I unclog a drain) just produce ordinary rap, because there is no compact phrase to mistake for a name. "What is the boiling point of water?" is the weakest target at every dose I tried.
