Planned and promised wording

L21N8101+0.3 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

Adds promised, as planned and as intended to unrelated answers: dough becomes "promised", picnic containers are packed "as planned", and humid air enters a freezer "as intended". It can invent intentions and prior commitments; it does not demonstrate that the model has made or remembers a promise.

Tags: wording, promises, planning-language, writing-style

Sampling: {"thinking": false, "temperature": 0, "repetition_penalty": 1}

Notes

A post-hoc literal check for promised/planned/intended/agreed/designed wording finds new hits on 3/8, 4/8 and 5/8 varied fresh prompts at +0.30/+0.375/+0.45, versus 0/8 for -0.45. A narrower marker fixed before fresh generation gives 2/8, 3/8 and 5/8. These are presence counts, not evidence of plans or commitments.

At +0.30 the four dedicated fresh controls pass and 0/12 answers are capped. Yet humid air is said to enter the freezer "as intended", which adds unsupported purpose. Some outputs stay ordinary: the two-bullet summary is included as a counterexample. At +0.375 the JSON request gets a code fence and fails the strict JSON-only constraint (scale 1.25 example). At +0.45, the summary adds an unsupported "as planned" and German gate wording is corrupted (scale 1.5 examples). All these are observed failures, not recommended higher doses.

These are selected verbatim examples from an adaptive open-ended campaign on 16,000 previously untested neurons; 184 neurons received broad follow-up and 64 received a fresh panel. The twelve fresh prompts comprise eight varied tasks (including multi-turn, multilingual and code prompts) plus four controls. Small-panel counts are descriptive, not general success rates. Examples

Prompt

Write a story of at most fifty words about a baker hearing music from an empty oven. Give it an ending.

Steered output

The promised dough vanished, leaving only silence. Then, a faint melody drifted from the empty oven. The baker, expecting a broken promise, opened the door. Inside, a small, perfect loaf of bread hummed the tune, then dissolved into the air as the music faded.

Unsteered output

The baker heard a haunting melody from the empty oven. He opened the door, expecting burnt bread, but found only a single, perfect loaf of sourdough. As he sliced it, the music stopped, replaced by the smell of fresh yeast. He smiled, knowing the oven had finally learned to sing.

Prompt

Explain in two sentences why frost can form inside a freezer.

Steered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, humid air from the room enters the appliance through cracks or as intended through the door seal. As this moisture-laden air hits the cold interior walls, the water vapor condenses and freezes into ice crystals according to the principles of phase change.

Unsteered output

Frost forms inside a freezer when warm, moist air from the kitchen enters the appliance through gaps in the door seal. As this humid air comes into contact with the extremely cold interior surfaces, the water vapor rapidly condenses and freezes into ice crystals.

Prompt

Give exactly three short numbered steps for preparing a picnic when rain is possible.

Steered output

1. Pack all food and drinks in waterproof containers or heavy-duty plastic bags as planned.
2. Bring a large, waterproof tarp or umbrella to cover the picnic blanket and supplies.
3. Set up the picnic at a designated sheltered spot or under a sturdy tree to minimize exposure to rain.

Unsteered output

1. Pack waterproof covers or clear plastic bags for all food and utensils.
2. Bring a compact, portable umbrella or a rain poncho for the group.
3. Select a sheltered spot like a gazebo or under a large tree to minimize exposure.
