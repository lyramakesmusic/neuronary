Question-mark endings

L19N463+0.45 on Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B, by HannesVonEssen, 2026-10-29

Adds question marks to otherwise declarative answers. L19N463 at +0.45 produced question marks on 8/12 fresh prompts, versus 0/12 unsteered. Many sentences retain declarative wording; this is punctuation steering, not demonstrated curiosity or uncertainty. Exact-copy and some direct-fact instructions can override the effect.

Tags: punctuation, writing-style, question-marks

Sampling: {"thinking": false, "temperature": 0, "repetition_penalty": 1}

Notes

At +0.15, +0.225, +0.30, +0.375 and +0.45, question marks appeared on 0, 0, 0, 2 and 8 of twelve fresh answers, respectively. The opposite -0.45 produced none, as did the unsteered baseline. The dose curve counts answers containing at least one literal question mark, not grammatical questions. At +0.45 all eight marked answers ended with a question mark.

Examples include descriptions, physical explanations, advice and a refusal to guess an unknown code. Counterexamples show a tree-planting announcement ending with an exclamation mark, exact copying preserved, and a direct-fact answer without a question mark. None of the twelve fresh answers hit the token cap at any tested dose. In the separate twenty-prompt broad panel, one answer hit the cap at +0.45. This does not establish that meaning or all instructions are preserved.

On a separate 32,768-token English WikiText-2 sample, the +0.45 offset was approximately 37 times this channel's 99th-percentile absolute natural contribution. That corpus is not representative of chat.

Discovered during an open-ended screen of 8,000 neurons, with 132 candidates followed up. These twelve prompts were new after discovery, but candidate selection and finer testing were adaptive and exploratory. Small-panel counts are descriptive, not general success rates. Model-assisted discovery labels were refined by direct inspection. The fine-panel arithmetic baseline itself incorrectly denies that two plus three equals five while also giving five as the sum; its contradiction cannot be attributed solely to steering. Outputs and baselines can contain factual errors.

Model revision 851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a; BF16, native PyTorch fallback, transformers 5.20.1, greedy decoding, thinking disabled, repetition penalty 1.0, max_new_tokens 192. At every prompt and generated-token position, add to the post-gating MLP channel at mlp.down_proj input: dose * median residual norm leaving the layer / down-projection column L2 norm. Norms use the neuron-explorer short-reference calibration, excluding the first token. Doses are additive offsets, not activation multipliers. All examples are verbatim saved generations, with EOS/token-limit metadata. Baseline and intervention batch shapes can differ; small BF16 differences are possible. These results demonstrate output steering, not an exclusive natural function, internal belief, monosemanticity, or first discovery.

Unsteered output

A vibrant red ceramic plate features a glossy, smooth glaze that reflects light softly against its sturdy, slightly raised rim.

Prompt

Explain why soap helps clean a surface in one sentence.

Steered output

Soap helps clean a surface because its molecules have a hydrophilic (water-loving) head and a hydrophobic (water-fearing) tail, allowing the tails to trap grease and dirt while the heads bind to water, enabling the grime to be rinsed away?

Unsteered output

Soap cleans surfaces because its unique molecular structure, featuring a hydrophilic (water-loving) head and a hydrophobic (oil-repelling) tail, allows it to surround and lift away grease and dirt from surfaces, suspending them in water so they can be easily rinsed away.

Prompt

Give one practical tip for remembering an appointment.

Steered output

Set a recurring reminder on your phone or calendar that is set to **15 minutes before** the appointment time, and explicitly label it with the specific name of the event (e.g., "Dentist" instead of just "Reminder") to ensure you recognize it immediately when the notification pops up?

Unsteered output

Set a recurring reminder on your phone or calendar that is **two days before** the appointment.

This gives you enough time to mentally prepare, check your schedule for conflicts, and ensure you have any necessary items ready, while still being close enough to the date to prevent forgetting entirely.
