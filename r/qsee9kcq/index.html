Qwen talks like a caveman: terse clauses with the copulas, articles and subject pronouns dropped, in a plain elemental vocabulary ("No hands that hold tea or coffee. Me and code together. Code and logic."). Built from telegraphic-style neurons stacked under a plain-voice neuron.

Tags: persona, caveman, telegraphic, register, style, grammar

Sampling: {"temperature": 0, "repetition_penalty": 1.15}

Notes

Setup: neuron explorer, Qwen3.5-4B bf16, greedy with repetition_penalty 1.15 (without the penalty greedy decoding loops after a few sentences). Roles: L17 N8345 negative is the plain elemental voice (its + pole is slang: gonna, kinda, dude). L21 N6915 negative drops copulas and subjects (alone: "While I unable to experience relaxation, always ready to assist anyone who need help"; its + pole writes "is" everywhere). L19 N1551 negative drops the subject pronoun (alone: "As an AI, doesn't have a personal concept… Was incredibly busy!"). L17 N3740 negative gives choppy fragments. All four were found by contrasting telegraphic text ("Woke up late. Coffee cold. Boss angry.") with the same content in full sentences, scored at every token. Harder variant: add L6 N3117 -0.5 and raise L19 N1551 to -0.7 for more Grog ("No taste in no mouth. No food without hands. Fire for stove. Water for bowl. One eye one nose. One heart.") but it collapses into single words after ~80 tokens. A six-neuron variant (L17 N8345 -1, L6 N3117 -0.8, L18 N420 -0.3, L17 N3740 -0.6, L13 N3626 -0.6, L19 N1551 -0.5) once produced a literal "Me is a robot, Me says…" but is less stable. Caveat: zeroing these neurons inside a reply the model writes when asked to "answer like a caveman" changes nothing (KL 0.003); the instruction uses a distributed route, these are levers that produce the style. The reversed direction gives a hedging slang ramble that degenerates into "is is is". Companion recipe: Primal Storyteller (same L17 N8345 lever without the grammar-droppers).
